Assistant Button 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

Assistant Button（套件名稱：tw.com.juying.assistantbutton）由 鉅穎資訊科技（JUYING INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY COMPANY）提供。 本政策說明本應用程式如何處理使用者資料。

資料收集與分享

Assistant Button 不會收集、儲存、傳送或分享個人資料，也不使用廣告、 分析追蹤、使用者帳戶或遠端伺服器。

無障礙服務

Assistant Button 使用 Android Accessibility Service，讓使用者可透過 Android 的無障礙快捷按鈕啟動裝置上的數位助理。 本應用程式不會讀取螢幕內容、不會監聽無障礙事件，也不會透過無障礙服務 收集、儲存或傳送任何個人資料。

第三方服務

當使用者啟動裝置上的數位助理時，該數位助理及其相關服務由其提供者管理， 並適用該提供者自己的隱私權政策。Assistant Button 不會取得數位助理處理的內容。

政策變更

若本應用程式的資料處理方式發生變更，本頁將同步更新。

Assistant Button Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Assistant Button (package tw.com.juying.assistantbutton) is provided by JUYING INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY COMPANY. This policy explains how the app handles user data.

Data collection and sharing

Assistant Button does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data. It does not use advertising, analytics tracking, user accounts, or remote servers.

Accessibility Service

Assistant Button uses the Android Accessibility Service so users can launch the device's digital assistant from the Android accessibility shortcut button. The app does not read screen content, monitor accessibility events, or collect, store, or transmit personal data through the Accessibility Service.

Third-party services

When the device's digital assistant is launched, that assistant and related services are operated by their respective providers and are subject to those providers' privacy policies. Assistant Button does not receive the content handled by the digital assistant.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app's data-handling practices change.
